X16 — first-person sight revision

Replaced only the four original sight islands inside FP_Slide_Mesh with a
wider open U-notch, white rear outline, and a taller dark front post carrying
a warm-white dot and white top index. The existing part names and transforms,
grip origin, gun length, other attachments, and all non-sight surfaces remain
unchanged. TP, casing and sound files are unchanged copies.

From the requested eye distance, perspective makes the post about half as
wide as the opening: approximately 15 versus 31 pixels at 960 x 540.
Post top and notch top align at model-local Roblox Y = 0.575.
